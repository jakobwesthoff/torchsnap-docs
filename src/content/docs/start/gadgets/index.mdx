Gadgets are the plugins that power everything Torchsnap can do. Every search
result, action, and custom view you see in the launcher is provided by a
gadget. Torchsnap ships with a set of built-in gadgets out of the box, and you
can [install additional ones](/start/settings/#gadgets) or
[build your own](/development/).

## How gadgets appear in the launcher

Gadgets surface their results in different ways:

**Plain search**: most gadgets contribute results alongside everything else
when you type. For example, the [App Launcher](/start/gadgets/app-launcher/)
and [System Commands](/start/gadgets/system-commands/) both respond to the same
query, and their results are ranked together by relevance and
[frecency](/start/settings/#frecency).

**Prefix routing**: some gadgets activate when you type a specific prefix
character. This lets you target a single gadget directly:

| Prefix | Gadget |
|---|---|
| `=` | [Calculator](/start/gadgets/calculator/): evaluate math expressions |
| `:` | [Emoji Picker](/start/gadgets/emoji-picker/): search emoji by shortcode |

**Inline detection**: some gadgets activate when they recognize a pattern
anywhere in the query. [Bangs](/start/gadgets/bangs/) trigger when `!` appears
at any position (e.g., `pantine !crates`),
[Open URL](/start/gadgets/open-url/) activates when you type a URL, and the
[Calculator](/start/gadgets/calculator/) also detects math expressions
automatically without requiring the `=` prefix.

**Custom views**: a few gadgets render their own UI beyond the standard result
list. The [Clipboard Manager](/start/gadgets/clipboard-manager/) shows a grid
of recent clipboard entries, and the
[Calculator](/start/gadgets/calculator/) displays the result inline above the
search bar.

## Managing gadgets

You can enable, disable, install, and uninstall gadgets in
[Settings](/start/settings/#gadgets). Some gadgets also have their own
configuration panels. These appear in the Settings sidebar under the gadget's
name.
